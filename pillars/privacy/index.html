Privacy Policy

Pillars is a personal iOS app by Sebastian Olmos. Effective date: 1 September 2026.

This page describes what the current App Store version of Pillars does with your information.

At a glance

  • Logs live on your iPhone (SwiftData). There is no Pillars account.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites (NSPrivacyTracking is false).
  • Apple Health is read-only. HealthKit data is not used for marketing or ads.
  • AI Coach is off in this version. Nothing is sent to a coach backend.

What Pillars is

Pillars lets you log workouts, keep a food and symptom diary, and view sleep and heart rate from Apple Health (HealthKit). It is a personal wellness log.

It is not a medical device, not diagnostic, and not a substitute for professional care. It does not count calories for you and it is not an AI doctor.

In-app pattern hints are rule-based guesses from your own logs. They are not allergy tests, diagnoses, or medical findings.

Data stored on your device

If you use the app, it may store on this device:

  • Workouts you log
  • Meals and symptoms you log
  • App settings (for example appearance)

That data is stored locally with SwiftData. This data stays on your device unless you export it yourself (CSV). A future version may add Coach; we will update this policy before any logs leave the device.

You can remove on-device data by deleting individual logs in the app, or by deleting the app. Data that lives in Apple Health stays in Apple Health.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you allow it, Pillars reads (and does not write) from Apple Health:

  • Sleep analysis
  • Heart rate
  • Resting heart rate

This is used only to show you your own recent rest next to your food and training logs. You can turn access off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

HealthKit data is not used for marketing, advertising, or tracking. We do not sell it. In this version it does not leave your device.

Food search (USDA)

When you search for a food, Pillars may send the search text to USDA FoodData Central (a public U.S. government API) so the app can look up foods. We send the search text you type, not your full food diary or Health data. USDA's own terms apply to that service. Search is optional; you can still type a meal yourself.

Tracking, ads, and sale of data

  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No advertising identifier use for ads
  • No sale of personal information
  • No sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising

California residents: we do not sell your personal information, and we do not use HealthKit data for marketing.

AI Coach (not in this version)

This version does not send your logs to a coach backend. Coach is not shipping.

If Coach is enabled in a later release, using it would send summarized local logs (meals, symptoms, workouts, and sleep summaries) to a developer-operated proxy, only when you send a Coach message, not in the background, and only to generate a reply. That data would not be used for ads or sold. We will name any language-model provider in this policy before Coach ships. You would not have to use Coach.

Children

Pillars is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will post the new version here and update the effective date.
